// The Process

HOW WE WORK

No surprises. No endless back-and-forth. No trailing work for months. A clear process from first call to deployed product. Here's exactly what working together looks like.

// The Journey

FROM IDEA TO PRODUCTION

01

DISCOVERY CALL

45 minutes

We talk about your vision, your users, and your constraints. I ask questions. You share context. By the end, we both know if this is a good fit. No pressure, no pitch — just a conversation.

02

PROJECT BRIEF

You complete async

I send you a structured brief to fill out. Features, priorities, inspirations, constraints. Takes about 30 minutes. This homework means we hit the ground running — no wasted time getting aligned.

03

STRATEGY SESSION

90 minutes

We map out exactly what we're building. Data model, user flows, integrations. You leave with a clear scope, timeline, and fixed price. No hourly guessing games. You know exactly what you're getting.

04

BUILD SPRINT

2–6 weeks

I build. You get daily async updates and weekly sync calls. You always know where we are. AI handles the boilerplate, I handle the architecture and quality. You see progress from day one.

05

SHIP

Day of completion

Deployed to production. Documentation handed off. Two weeks of bug fixes included. You own everything — code, domain, accounts. No vendor lock-in. No trailing work. Done means done.

// The Approach

HOW AI CHANGES EVERYTHING

70% AI-GENERATED

AI writes 70–90% of boilerplate code. Authentication, CRUD operations, standard integrations — all generated in minutes instead of hours. This is where the speed comes from.

15+ YEARS REVIEWING

AI alone produces Frankenstein code. Every line gets reviewed by someone with 15+ years of production experience. You get speed without sacrificing quality or security.

80% SAME PATTERNS

Every Rails app is 80% the same: auth, admin, jobs, emails, deployment. I've built these patterns dozens of times. The 20% that's unique is where I focus my expertise.

3× FASTER DELIVERY

What takes traditional agencies 4–8 months, I deliver in 2–6 weeks. Same quality, fraction of the time. Your product launches while competitors are still in discovery.
